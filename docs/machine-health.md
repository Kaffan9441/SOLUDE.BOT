## Content treatment

The service covers vibration, acoustic, temperature, edge analytics, and integration with operational and maintenance workflows. The five evaluation dimensions from the brochure are measurement, intelligence, integration, robustness, and economics. Detailed considerations include sampling, frequency response, raw data, validation, connectivity, environmental fit, and lifecycle cost.

The source image's interface labels are expressed as BACnet/IP, Modbus TCP/IP, MQTT over TLS, and HTTPS. They are explicitly options to scope for the selected system, not a guarantee of support by unspecified hardware. No sensor model, vendor partnership, diagnostic accuracy, certified standard compliance, guaranteed downtime reduction, or autonomous maintenance action is asserted. No numeric measurements or fabricated live dashboard are displayed.
