// DESIGN FOR THE WHOLE LIFECYCLE

Control. Protect. Maintain.

Define the engineering practices and deliverables your project needs before selecting the implementation.

01OT cybersecurity architecture

Scope Purdue-aligned segmentation and ISA/IEC 62443 zones and conduits. Define industrial firewalls, controlled remote access, MFA, asset inventory, patch governance, and backup ownership against the site risk assessment.

02Functional and machine safety

Start with hazard and risk assessment, then document safety requirements, design, verification, and validation. Scope safety PLCs, safety networks, scanners, interlocks, and proof testing where applicable.

03Reliability and high availability

Assess redundant PLCs, hot standby, controller failover, server and network redundancy, historian replication, and monitored diagnostics. Agree on backup, restore, and disaster-recovery objectives and verify recovery procedures.

04PLC engineering and migration governance

Use control narratives, FDS, state machines, alarm conventions, and handshake matrices with version control and simulation tests. Plan cutover, rollback, and acceptance criteria for PLC-5 / SLC500 to ControlLogix, S7-300 to S7-1500, or other platform migrations as applicable.

05Historian and data governance

Define asset models, naming, timestamps, data quality, raw versus processed access, storage, retention, buffering, and replication. Assign ownership for interfaces and changes from the field layer to enterprise applications.

06Maintainability and operating support

Establish asset inventories, lifecycle and obsolescence plans, controlled change and release procedures, operator training, and turnover documentation. Agree on hypercare, escalation paths, and ongoing support responsibilities.
